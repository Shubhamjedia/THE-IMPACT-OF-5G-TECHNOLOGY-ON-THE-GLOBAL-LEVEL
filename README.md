# THE-IMPACT-OF-5G-TECHNOLOGY-ON-THE-GLOBAL-LEVEL

## 📄 Overview & Abstract
This repository hosts the peer-reviewed research paper titled **"The Impact of 5G Technology on the Global Level"**, published in the *International Research Journal of Modernization in Engineering Technology and Science (IRJMETS)* (Volume 07, Issue 03, March 2025). 

The study comprehensively explores how 5G wireless technology revolutionizes global connectivity, speed, ultra-low latency, and shapes emerging digital ecosystems across industries such as healthcare, media, and IoT.

## 📌 Key Research Highlights
* **Sector Transformations:** Evaluates 5G deployment in healthcare (telemedicine, remote surgery), media (4K/8K streaming, AR/VR), and customer experiences.
* **IoT & Automation:** Analyzes 5G as a primary accelerator for the Internet of Things (IoT), smart cities, and industrial automation.
* **Security & Challenges:** Addresses infrastructure investments (small cells, fiber-optics), regulatory spectrum management, and cybersecurity/data privacy risks.
* **Global Statistics:** Reviews worldwide 5G adoption milestones, speeds, and regional growth metrics (highlighting rapid expansion in India).
